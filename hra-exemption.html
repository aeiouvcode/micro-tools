Which amount is exempt?

The smallest of actual HRA, 40% of salary (50% if the rented home is in Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata or Chennai), and rent paid minus 10% of salary. Here salary means basic plus qualifying DA and turnover-based commission. No rent means no exemption. This is an old-regime estimate, not tax filing advice.
