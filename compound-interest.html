How contributions compound

This is an equivalent-growth model. Monthly contributions arrive at each month's start; both principal and contributions earn an equivalent monthly rate derived from the nominal annual rate and chosen compounding frequency. For partial compounding periods, it uses fractional-period growth, not an actual bank interest-credit schedule. Duration must equal a whole number of months, up to 100 years. The final row includes partial years.

A note on rates

Enter the nominal annual rate, not an effective annual yield. Assumes a constant non-negative rate, no withdrawals, taxes, fees, daily date conventions or rounding of each credit. Confirm actual product rules before using the estimate.
