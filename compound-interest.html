How contributions compound

The principal compounds at the chosen frequency. Monthly contributions are added at the start of each month and earn the equivalent monthly rate derived from the effective annual yield - so daily compounding with monthly additions stays exact rather than approximated.

A note on rates

FD and RD rates in India typically compound quarterly; savings accounts compound daily but credit monthly. Matching the frequency to the product matters more than most people think.
