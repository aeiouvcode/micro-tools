JSON Formatter & Validator

Paste JSON, pick an action. Errors show the exact line and column.

Why a private JSON tool?

API responses and config files often contain tokens, emails or internal URLs. Most online formatters send your paste to a server. This one never does - parsing happens in your browser, on your device, and the page works offline.

What the validator catches

Trailing commas, unquoted keys, single quotes, missing brackets - the error message includes the line and column of the first problem so you can fix it fast.

Runs entirely on your device · No uploads · No tracking
