What the entropy number means

Entropy = log2 of the total number of possible passwords. Every added bit doubles the work an attacker must do. Below 45 bits is weak against an offline attack; 70+ bits is strong for anything short of state-level adversaries.

Why this generator is honest

Randomness comes from your browser's crypto module, and characters are picked with rejection sampling - no modulo bias, which quietly weakens many online generators. Crack-time estimates assume an offline attacker at 10 billion guesses per second and an online one at 100. Everything happens on your device; the password is never transmitted or stored.
