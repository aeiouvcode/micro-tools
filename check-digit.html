What does a valid check digit mean?

It catches many typing mistakes, but does not prove an identifier is assigned, genuine or tied to a specific product or device. GTIN/ISBN-13 use alternating 3 and 1 weights from the right; IMEI uses Luhn. See GS1's manual method.
