How calendar age is counted

Completed months are measured from your birth date, clamping to the last day when a month is shorter. Twelve completed months make a year. A February 29 birthday uses February 28 in non-leap years here; official or legal rules may differ. The next birthday is strictly after the selected date. Totals count calendar days; hours are days × 24, not elapsed clock hours across time-zone changes.
