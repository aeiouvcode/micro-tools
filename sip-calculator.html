How SIP returns are estimated

Each monthly instalment compounds at the expected annual rate divided monthly. Future value = P × [((1+i)n − 1) ÷ i] × (1+i), where i is the monthly rate and n the number of months.

A note on expectations

Market returns vary year to year; a flat rate is only an estimate. This tool does not account for expense ratios, exit loads or taxes. All math runs on your device - nothing is uploaded.
