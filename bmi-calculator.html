Why two sets of cutoffs?

WHO categories call 25+ overweight and 30+ obese. For South Asian bodies, health risks rise earlier, so Indian guidelines (MoHFW/consensus) flag 23+ as overweight and 27.5+ as obese. This tool shows both.

What BMI Prime is

BMI Prime is your BMI divided by 25 - the upper healthy bound. 1.00 is exactly the boundary, below 1 is under or inside the healthy zone. BMI is a screening number, not a diagnosis; it does not see muscle, bone or distribution.
