Categories and action points

WHO adult categories use BMI 25 for overweight and 30 for obesity. A 2004 WHO expert consultation kept these international categories and identified 23, 27.5, 32.5 and 37.5 as potential additional public-health action points for Asian populations. This page shows the first two (23 and 27.5). These are not universal Asian or Indian diagnostic cutoffs. This page shows whether you have reached each action point, not a second obesity diagnosis.

For adults only; not a child growth assessment or a pregnancy weight guide. BMI is a screening measure, not an individual diagnosis.

Sources: WHO adult categories; WHO expert consultation (2004). Checked 1 October 2026.

What BMI Prime is

BMI Prime is your BMI divided by 25 - the upper healthy bound. 1.00 is exactly the boundary, below 1 is under or inside the healthy zone. BMI is a screening number, not a diagnosis; it does not see muscle, bone or distribution.
