    What cron dialect does this use?

    Debian/Vixie-style five-field subset. Day fields use AND when either begins with *, including */2; otherwise they use OR. For example, 0 0 */2 * MON means Mondays on odd dates, not all Mondays plus odd dates. Cron dialects differ: this is not a universal scheduler validator. No seconds, years, L, W, #, ?, macros or Quartz extensions. Single-value steps such as 5/10 are refused: use an explicit range. Times use your browser's local zone, not a chosen server zone. This is a local civil-time preview, not a simulation of daemon catch-up, missed jobs or duplicate runs during daylight-saving changes. Test on your actual scheduler before deployment.

    Does it contact a server?

    No. Parsing and previews run entirely in your browser.
