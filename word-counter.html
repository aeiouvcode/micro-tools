About this word counter

Count words, characters (with and without spaces), sentences and paragraphs in real time. Reading time assumes 200 words per minute; use it for essays, articles, captions and scripts.

Is my text uploaded anywhere?

No. Every count happens on your device. Nothing is sent to a server, saved, or tracked - you can even use this page offline once loaded.

Runs entirely on your device · No uploads · No tracking
