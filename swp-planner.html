What is assumed?

A constant effective annual return converted into monthly growth; growth occurs before each month-end withdrawal. The withdrawal rises at each new year. If a full month's withdrawal is unaffordable, the model stops without a partial withdrawal. Markets do not return a fixed rate: volatility, tax, fees and sequence-of-returns risk can make real depletion earlier. This is a scenario model, not advice.
