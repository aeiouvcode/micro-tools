Why this diff?

Myers shortest edit script finds a minimal set of line insertions and deletions. It is line-based rather than word-based, so a changed line shows as one deletion and one addition. Long files are capped at 1,800 combined lines to keep a phone responsive.

Where does the text go?

Nowhere. The comparison runs locally with no uploads.
