What is the effective rate?

It is the annual compounded rate implied by the net amount you receive and all scheduled monthly EMIs. Unlike a nominal rate, it reflects the fee withheld at disbursal. It is an effective yield, not a lender's legally disclosed APR; fee classifications and regulatory methods may differ. The Consumer Financial Protection Bureau explains why APR can include charges beyond the stated loan interest rate.
