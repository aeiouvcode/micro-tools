SI vs IEC data units

Storage makers use SI units (1 KB = 1000 bytes); operating systems often display IEC units (1 KiB = 1024 bytes). This converter keeps both families separate and exact - the difference is why a "500 GB" drive shows about "465 GiB".

How conversions are computed

Every unit converts through an exact base-unit factor (metre, gram, second, byte...). Temperature uses the proper offset formulas rather than naive multiplication. All math runs on your device.
