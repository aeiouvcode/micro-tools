Add vs remove GST

Adding: GST = base × slab. Removing (when a price already includes GST): base = gross ÷ (1 + slab), GST = gross − base. The second is the one shop bills need, and the one most calculators get sloppy with.

CGST, SGST and IGST

Within a state, GST splits equally into CGST and SGST. Across states, the full amount is IGST. The total tax is the same - only who collects it changes. Slabs shown are the real notified rates, including 0.25% and 3% (precious metals and stones).
