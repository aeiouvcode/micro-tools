Why is take-home below CTC/12?

CTC can include employer-side PF, accrued gratuity or insurance, which are not paid as monthly cash. Payslip deductions such as employee PF, professional tax and TDS then reduce cash pay. EPFO describes ordinary PF contributions as 12% each of qualifying basic wages plus DA, but actual limits and enrollment can vary, so this tool asks for your own PF figure rather than guessing. There is no state-specific PT schedule or automatic income-tax calculation here.
