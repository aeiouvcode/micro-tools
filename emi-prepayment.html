Which option saves more?

Keeping the old EMI usually shortens the loan and saves more interest; lowering EMI frees monthly cash. This model assumes a fixed rate, monthly interest, immediate principal reduction after the chosen EMI, and no fees, taxes or rate changes. Check your lender's actual schedule and prepayment terms before acting.
