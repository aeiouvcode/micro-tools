What tax rules are included?

AY 2026-27 slabs for resident individuals under 60, old regime and Section 115BAC new regime, Section 87A rebate (new regime up to ₹12 lakh taxable income and marginal relief above), plus 4% health and education cess. The rebate only applies to eligible ordinary slab-rate income. The Income Tax Department's published tax chart is the source. No surcharge because this tool blocks ₹50 lakh and above.

Is this a filing calculation?

No. It excludes deductions eligibility, special-rate income, TDS, rounding conventions, rebate restrictions on special income and all nonstandard cases. Consult a tax professional for filing. The amounts stay on your device, without uploads.
